# Reel audio

`bed30_surf.wav` is the scored music bed for the Marine Nav app-preview reel — a light
San-Francisco surf theme, matching the app's coastal subject and its chart-paper daylight look.

Generated on device with the MLX port of **Stable Audio 3** (`sm-music` DiT, `same-s` decoder,
30 s, 8 steps) — see `marketing/reels/README.md` §"Generate the music bed" for the procedure.
Prompt and seed for every bed live in the `.txt` beside it: a bed without its prompt cannot be
regenerated, and re-rolling a seed does not reproduce a lost file.

**Never overwrite a bed that has been scored into a released reel.** Add a new one beside it.
