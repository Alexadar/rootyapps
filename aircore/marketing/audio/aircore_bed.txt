Why this brief suits the app
----------------------------
The design system is called **water-breeze** and the product is an air-side calculator, so an
airy, open-windowed instrumental is on-theme rather than at odds with it. The tempo is unhurried
on purpose: the preview shows a technician solving a state, not a game.

Method
------
Followed par/marketing/audio/par_bed.txt exactly — AudioSparx tags first, stacked genres, named
instruments, vivid prose about what each one plays, a stated BPM, a negative prompt, 100 steps,
and loudnorm at the end. That file records five earlier batches rejected on method rather than
taste, so none of it was improvised.

Do not bake fades in: store_preview.py applies its own fade-in/out and volume at mux time.
Never overwrite a bed already scored into a released preview — add a new file beside it.
