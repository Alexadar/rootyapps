Rooms are geometric placeholders — identical geometry before/after by construction, which is the promise; real renders drop in 1:1.

Model reality & platform honesty

  • No segmentation, no measuring — yet. The model diffuses the whole frame, conditioned on depth. So: no region picking, no dimension callouts, and no copy that claims metric accuracy. Depth conditioning still holds walls, windows and proportions — that stays the headline. Per-object edits and real dimensions are roadmap, and nothing here paints the UI into a corner for them.
  • Minutes of background Neural Engine work isn't guaranteed — iOS may suspend it. The Live Activity never fakes progress; suspension reads "Waiting for you", completion is a local notification (1e, 1h).
  • Thermal throttling is observed, not predicted — time-left is a rolling estimate from measured step duration.

Direction — how the prompt works

  • Presets are prompt macros: picking Scandinavian writes a full prompt into the field; the field stays editable. Structured first, free text second — a novice never has to type, an enthusiast can say anything. Refinement chips append to it.
  • The prompt is stored with the result and is the "regenerate from this" seed — same metadata contract as the wallpaper app.
  • Photo check folded in up top: depth badge + Retake. With no measuring there's nothing else a separate Space screen would earn.
  • Time is priced before commitment — the CTA says ~6 min; nobody is surprised by a wait they chose.
  • Exterior mode swaps presets (Modern farmhouse, Georgian, Mediterranean, Minimal render).

The multi-minute state

  • The forming image is the progress bar: latents decode every 2–3 steps under the milk veil (rgba(255,255,255,.22), blur 26→0 as steps advance) — the wallpaper app's forming-image spec, unchanged.
  • Named stages, real steps (`GenerationProgress` is step-based by contract): Reading the space → Composing → Refining → Full resolution. Never a bare spinner, never a fake percent.
  • Time-left is a rolling estimate from measured step duration, so it self-corrects under throttling.
  • Cancel is scoped — the queue survives. Cancel plays the morph in reverse, per the wallpaper spec.

Recommendation: the wipe slider — and why

  • Slider wins because the promise is geometric fidelity: a wipe lets the eye track one edge (window frame, wall line) across the divider and see it not move. A toggle tests memory; side-by-side halves the image and hides misalignment. Both survive as secondary gestures — tap to flip, hold to peek — and side-by-side becomes the variant-vs-variant view on iPad/Mac (1k, 1l).
  • "Try again" re-runs with the same prompt, new seed; "···" holds Share, Regenerate with edits (back to 1c with the prompt loaded), Delete.
  • VoiceOver: the slider is an adjustable element — swipe up/down moves 10%: "After revealed, 60 percent"; double-tap flips before/after.
