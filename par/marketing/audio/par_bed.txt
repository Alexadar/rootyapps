How to prompt this model — read docs/guides/prompting.md, not guesswork
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Five earlier batches were rejected. Every failure was method, and the fixes are all documented in
~/Projects/AudioProto/reference/stable-audio-3/docs/guides/prompting.md:

1. **Lead with AudioSparx tags.** `TrackType: Music, VocalType: Instrumental` is called out in the
   guide as producing "higher quality, more semantically coherent outputs". Then `Genre:` (stackable
   — `Genre: Funk, Genre: Jazz` is valid) and `Instruments:`.
2. **State the BPM.** The guide lists it as a key element. Omitting it lets the model choose, and it
   chose badly every time.
3. **Write vivid, specific prose** about what each instrument plays and how the room sounds. The
   guide's own examples are a paragraph long. Strings of mood adjectives give the model nothing.
4. **Use a negative prompt** (marketing/reels/README.md: "worth more than extra steps").
5. **Always loudnorm.** Raw generation is inconsistent and clips; every un-normalised take measured
   between 595 and 39,326 clipped samples.
6. **Steps: 100.** The house number. sa3_mlx.py's --help says the denoiser is distilled for 8 and
   ">8 gives diminishing returns and may overshoot" — 128 was past both and sounded worse.

The difference is measurable, not a matter of taste: the last badly-prompted bed came out at
636 Hz centroid and 11.6 dB crest — dark and flat. Same model, same steps, correctly formed prompt:
4927 Hz and 15.1 dB.

Do not bake fades in: store_preview.py applies its own fade-in/out and volume at mux time.
Never overwrite a bed already scored into a released preview — add a new file beside it.
