# 2026-08-08 — Kerf 1.0.3 ASO overhaul (first big lever)

## What was done
- Shipped Kerf 1.0.3: renamed to "Kerf Construction Calculator" (added the head noun — it had shipped effectively invisible), rewrote indexed keywords against real autocomplete phrases, added captioned store media.

## Baseline before
- 12 days pre: 34 impressions WW, 4 US, 2 page views, 0 downloads, 0 sales. Effectively undiscovered.

## Effect (measured 2026-08-16)
- US impressions 4 → 231 in 12 days (**58×**), 96% from search. Sales still 0 (see 2026-08-16 entry).

## Context
- Apple Ads Basic had started 2026-08-06 on Storypole + Marine Nav ($50/mo each) — NOT Kerf, so Kerf's lift is organic ASO.
- This is the action that established the working thesis: indexed metadata is load-bearing, and impressions are the binding constraint below which nothing else matters.
