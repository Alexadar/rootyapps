## Audit findings

- **Head noun: PRESENT.** "calculator" is spelled in full in the subtitle — Overtone Lab does *not*
  have Kerf Calc's abbreviation trap. But a second head noun, **"meter", is ABSENT**, and two target
  queries ("lufs meter", "loudness meter") are exactly that shape → structurally unreachable.
- **Missing high-intent atoms** (each kills every phrase containing it): `room`, `rt60`, `loudness`,
  `meter`, `speaker`, `box`, `subwoofer`, `string`, `tension`, `time`. Note `lufs` is present but
  `loudness` is not — they are different atoms; only the description carries "loudness", and the
  description **is not indexed for iOS search**.
- **Duplication: none.** Name/subtitle/keywords currently share no words. Clean.
- **Unused characters:** name **12/30** — 18 wasted in the heaviest-weighted field; subtitle 28/30;
  keywords 100/100 (full, but ~30 chars go to `ortf,fletcher,sabine,partch` — our own *tool* names,
  which are terms of art rather than things a buyer types).
- **Locales:** en-US only. es-MX is also indexed on the US storefront → ~160 free characters
  (30 name + 30 subtitle + 100 keywords) currently forfeited.
- **Promotional text** is correctly used as conversion copy, not keyword space.
- **4.3(b) differentiators** (validated math / offline / buy-once) appear in the description but
  **not** in name or subtitle. True in the code doesn't count at review.
- **No StoreKit at all** → the IAP-display-name search surface is forfeited (a consequence of
  shipping no IAPs, not of being paid). In-app Events remain free and unused.

### ⚠️ Sequencing trap (found during this audit)
**Name and subtitle live on `appInfo`, which is app-level and shared by both platforms — not
per-version.** Overtone Lab's editable `appInfo` is currently in **WAITING_FOR_REVIEW**, attached to
the iOS 1.0 submission. Editing name/subtitle now means pulling iOS out of review and losing its
queue position, on a submission that has already been rejected once and re-fixed. **Keywords are
per-version and carry no such cost.**

Recommended order:
1. **Now:** keywords → macOS 1.0.1 only. Submit whenever you're ready.
2. **After iOS 1.0 is approved:** apply name + subtitle + es-MX, and ship them with iOS 1.0.1.
   Nothing is lost by waiting — the iOS SKU isn't indexed until it's released anyway.

Get the iOS build approved and released. Every iOS position above is "NOT FOUND" for the simple
reason that the app isn't on that store yet — no amount of metadata work changes that, and the iPhone
storefront is where the searches in this niche happen.
