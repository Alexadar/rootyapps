Every locale's App Store description must end with an entertainment disclaimer. The owner's
framing, adapted to App Review-safe language: we take no responsibility for what the cards
show; the app faithfully reproduces the classical reading model used through the ages.

English master (translate per locale in the metadata phase, same register):

> Tarot is for reflection and entertainment. The app faithfully recreates the classical
> card-reading tradition practiced for centuries — it does not predict the future, and its
> readings are not advice of any kind. What the cards show is yours to interpret; the app
> and its makers take no responsibility for decisions made from a reading. Interpretations
> are written on your device by Apple Intelligence and never leave it.

Register notes:
- Keep "entertainment/reflection" first — the standard fortune-teller-app defense and

  dropped the "distinguished magi" flourish — App Review reads occult grandiosity badly,
  and the description must never promise supernatural efficacy (4.0/1.4.1 adjacent).

- NEVER use the trademarked deck name anywhere in metadata; the deck is "Classic 1909".
